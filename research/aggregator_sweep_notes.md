# Global aggregator sweep notes (working file)

The sweep reads the global aggregators, the sources publishing one series for many countries, which serve every regional search at once. Jason's instruction of 14 October 2026 puts every aggregator connector in the build. The regional aggregators sit in the regional notes, research/phase5_lac/lac_round1_notes.md, research/phase6_sas/sas_round1_notes.md and research/phase7_eap/eap_round1_notes.md. The sweep started on 14 October 2026, and every panel figure below comes from data/panel/hiv_panel.csv as read on 14 October 2026 (229,274 rows).

## Rounds of the UNAIDS estimates in the panel
- unaids_aidsinfo reads the AIDSinfo exports in data/raw/unaids, retrieved on 5 September 2026. The year columns of the AIDSinfo exports run from 1990 to 2024.
- who_gho reads the WHO Global Health Observatory (GHO) and carries values for 2025 on people living with HIV, new infections and AIDS deaths.
- UNICEF (the United Nations Children's Fund) labels every modelled value of the UNICEF HIV_AIDS dataset "UNAIDS 2026 estimates", with values for 2010 to 2025.
- who_gho and unaids_aidsinfo share 3,442 country-years of people living with HIV in the panel, and the who_gho value differs from the AIDSinfo value by more than half a per cent in 2,220 of the 3,442 country-years. Madagascar 2024 reads 130,000 people living with HIV in who_gho and 90,000 in AIDSinfo. The differences fit who_gho carrying the 2026 round beside the 2025 round of the AIDSinfo exports.
- AIDSinfo now serves the 2026 round (VERIFIED by HEAD requests of 14 October 2026 to aidsinfo.unaids.org/public/documents/). Estimates_2026_en.zip (23,188,610 bytes), GAM_2026_en.zip (1,274,156 bytes) and KPAtlasDB_2026_en.zip (971,015 bytes) all carry a last-modified date of 23 September 2026, 18 days after the AIDSinfo exports in data/raw/unaids were retrieved. Estimates_2025_en.zip (26,616,806 bytes) and NCPI_2025_en.zip (2,994,865 bytes) still answer. The three 2026 zip files are a download for Jason to approve, and unaids_aidsinfo reads the 2025 round until Jason approves the download and runs the connector on the 2026 files.
- reconcile.py infers the who_gho and unaids_aidsinfo relationship from the data, because DECLARED_RELATIONSHIPS holds no who_gho and unaids_aidsinfo entry for people living with HIV, new infections or AIDS deaths.
- A series joining values from two rounds can report a change in the wrong direction (the Madagascar PMTCT coverage example under connector 29 below). Connector 29 therefore carries the whole series of the 2026 round and records the round in extra.

## UNICEF HIV_AIDS dataset, connector 29 (unicef_hiv)
- Access, GRADE 1. UNICEF publishes the dataset UNICEF,HIV_AIDS,1.0 through a public SDMX (Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange) service at https://sdmx.data.unicef.org/ws/public/sdmxapi/rest/, with no sign-in and no key. The dataset has seven dimensions (REF_AREA, INDICATOR, AGE, SEX, WEALTH_QUINTILE, RESIDENCE and DATA_SOURCE) and 19 HIV indicators. A request key with the wrong number of parts answers HTTP 404 with the text "No data for data query", so a 404 from the UNICEF service is not proof that a series is empty.
- PMTCT (prevention of mother-to-child transmission) values read on 14 October 2026. HVA_PMTCT_ARV_CVG (pregnant women with HIV who received antiretroviral medicine for PMTCT, per cent) holds 1,365 country values for 2010 to 2025 under DATA_SOURCE "Global AIDS Monitoring and UNAIDS 2026 estimates", with a lower and an upper bound on every value. 174 coverage values read ">95" and one coverage value reads "<1". HVA_PMTCT_MTCT (the vertical transmission rate, HIV-exposed infants who acquire HIV per 100) holds 1,371 country values for 2010 to 2025 under DATA_SOURCE "UNAIDS 2026 estimates", with both bounds on every value and no censored value.
- Against the AIDSinfo exports. The UNICEF PMTCT answer covers 88 countries for coverage and 86 for vertical transmission. 2,242 of the 2,736 UNICEF PMTCT values fall on a country and year AIDSinfo also holds, and 1,667 of the 2,242 differ by more than half a point or 2 per cent, whichever is larger. The other 494 UNICEF PMTCT values hold no AIDSinfo counterpart, 239 coverage values over 84 countries and 255 vertical transmission values over 85 countries, and 167 of the 494 are for 2025. ADDITIVE.
- Countries with no AIDSinfo PMTCT value in any year. Azerbaijan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Jamaica, Mauritius, Nigeria, Trinidad and Tobago, Uruguay and Venezuela hold no AIDSinfo value for coverage or for vertical transmission. Uzbekistan holds no AIDSinfo coverage value, and Australia and Liberia hold no AIDSinfo vertical transmission value. Jamaica's UNICEF vertical transmission series stops at 2022.
- Single years missing from AIDSinfo. Algeria lacks AIDSinfo values for 2020 to 2024 on both PMTCT indicators, Bolivia and Libya lack 2024 coverage, Colombia and the Netherlands lack 2024 vertical transmission, and Somalia lacks 2023 and 2024 vertical transmission.
- Why connector 29 carries the whole series. Madagascar's PMTCT coverage reads 30 for 2024 in AIDSinfo, while the 2026 round reads 20 for 2024 and 27 for 2025. Joining AIDSinfo 2024 to UNICEF 2025 shows a fall of three points, and the 2026 round alone shows a rise of seven points. 2025 is the first year after the cuts to United States funding, so the change from 2024 to 2025 has to come from one round. Connector 29 emits the full 2026-round series under source_id unicef_hiv, and the panel keeps the AIDSinfo figure beside the UNICEF figure.
- reconcile.DECLARED_RELATIONSHIPS declares the unaids_aidsinfo and unicef_hiv pair derived for pmtct_coverage_pct and for vertical_transmission_pct, so a revision between UNAIDS rounds never counts as two measurements disagreeing.
- UNICEF series refused as DUPLICATE. HVA_PED_ART_CVG (children's ART coverage) equals who_gho in all 1,364 shared values. HVA_PED_ART_NUM (children on ART) and HVA_PMTCT_ARV_NUM (pregnant women receiving antiretroviral medicine) repeat the who_gho and unaids_aidsinfo counts rounded to two significant figures, for example Thailand 2023 children on ART at 1,200 in UNICEF against 1,175 in who_gho. The seven survey indicators (HVA_PREV_*) hold no panel indicator (research/phase5_lac/lac_round1_notes.md, the UNICEF SDMX section).
- Status. Connector 29 was BUILT on 14 October 2026 (hivimpact/connectors/unicef_hiv.py), registered in hivimpact/connectors/__init__.py and catalogued in scripts/build_site.py. The offline test passed on the saved API answer of 14 October 2026 with the network blocked, giving 1,365 coverage rows and 1,371 vertical transmission rows with no repeated key. - Two ceilings on PMTCT coverage. UNICEF publishes a coverage above 95 as ">95", and the AIDSinfo 2025 export publishes a coverage above 98 as ">98". In the panel of 14 October 2026, unicef_hiv holds 179 coverage values of 95, 174 marked ">95" in extra.censored and five exact, and no value above 95. unaids_aidsinfo holds 141 coverage values of 98, 120 marked ">98" and 21 exact, and 35 values between 95 and 98. 95 of the 139 country-years where unicef_hiv reads 95 read 98 in unaids_aidsinfo. The unicef_hiv and unaids_aidsinfo connectors keep the censored number as the value and as the lower bound, so a comparison of the two sources above 95 compares two floors and not two estimates.

## UNAIDS Inequalities dashboard (aidsinfo.unaids.org/inequalities), connector 32 (unaids_phia)

- Content. 18 indicators drawn from four kinds of source.
