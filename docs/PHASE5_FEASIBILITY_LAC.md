# Phase 5. Feasibility log for HIV data sources in Latin America and the Caribbean

The Phase 5 log records the search for HIV data sources in the 23 countries of the Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) region of hivimpact/config/regions.py. The search ran in two rounds from 6 October 2026 to 8 October 2026 under the method of docs/PHASE4_FEASIBILITY_WCA.md and the grades of docs/ACCESS_LADDER.md. Round one read the sources of every country and swept source classes across the region. Round two retried the hosts with no answer in round one, applied the ACCESS_LADDER §8e stop rule, and ran two download passes under Jason's permission for all necessary downloads. The working notes of the search sit in research/phase5_lac/lac_round1_notes.md with the downloaded files in research/phase5_lac/dl, and every number below cites a numbered line of the working notes.

## The gap the search set out to fill

The panel holds no national surveillance series of new HIV diagnoses for any LAC country (notes line 7). The panel new_diagnoses rows for LAC come from programme counts of PEPFAR (the United States President's Emergency Plan for AIDS Relief) and the Global Fund (the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria) only, and for Brazil in 2024 the PEPFAR count of 1,156 sits against a PAHO national count of 39,216 (notes lines 4 to 5).

## The regional layer, PAHO

- Core Indicator 93. PAHO (the Pan American Health Organization) publishes Core Indicator 93, the count of new HIV diagnoses reported by every ministry of health, through a public querydata API behind the PAHO Core Indicators reports (notes line 772). Core Indicator 93 covers 22 of the 23 LAC countries, with series from 1995 for five countries and no row for Haiti (notes line 773). Core Indicator 93 is ADDITIVE for all 22 countries under a separate indicator of notified HIV cases, never under new_diagnoses, because the panel new_diagnoses rows count positive tests in programmes, and Jason ruled on 8 October 2026 for a separate indicator id of notified HIV cases (notes lines 777, 2063 and 2065).
- Haiti. The PAHO Interactive HIV Data Dashboard carries Haitian rows of new diagnoses for 2018 to 2024 under a provider string naming the Guyanese national AIDS programme (notes line 13). Which PAHO table is the source of the Haitian rows is NOT SETTLED (notes line 776).
- Other PAHO tables. A table of people on ART (antiretroviral therapy) by treatment line and on dolutegravir is ADDITIVE, because the panel holds no regimen indicator (notes line 16). The PAHO table of people on ART republishes the GAM (Global AIDS Monitoring) figures of UNAIDS (the Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS) (notes line 15). The PAHO table of PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis) adds only the year 2025 and unrounded values to the UNAIDS rows (notes line 33). The PAHO HIV mortality rates come from the modelled Global Health Estimates of WHO (the World Health Organization), NEGATIVE (notes line 23).

## Where the search stopped

Every outcome of the round-two search passes fitted a source class known from round one, and round two changed no rule about what counts as a source, so the LAC search met the ACCESS_LADDER §8e stop condition (notes lines 1908 and 1934). The next LAC step after the stop check was a download pass chosen by Jason, and the download passes of 7 October 2026 and 8 October 2026 follow below.

## Decisions of 12 October 2026

Jason answered the open questions of the draft on 12 October 2026 by adopting the recommendations below (notes line 2257).

- OCR. No figure read by OCR enters the panel, and the ACCESS_LADDER rule against OCR of scanned PDFs stands, because a subtotal test checks the sum of a block and leaves the split between rows untested (notes line 2258).
- MSyD request. Jason sends a request to the Bolivian ministry for the closing bulletins of 2020 to 2025 or for SIMONE extracts, and the draft of the request sits in research/phase5_lac/msyd_request_draft.md (notes line 2259).
- UNAIDS edition. Jason re-exports the 146 AIDSinfo files and reruns the panel, because the panel unaids_aidsinfo rows sit an estimates round behind in every region (notes line 2260).
- sica.int. The search makes no use of Wayback Machine copies, because an archived copy would route around the live F5 block, and COMISCA stays BLOCKED (notes line 2261).
- Brazil budget. The search sends no further query to orcamento.dados.gov.br, and the 2025 difference between the SPARQL and portal paid totals stays NOT SETTLED (notes line 2262).
- Registered-death routes. The request to ABS or STATIN for a list of dataZoa tables and the WHO Mortality Database class sweep wait on the source class ruling for registered deaths (notes line 2263).
- Scope. The LAC list keeps the 23 countries of regions.py. The World Bank country API read on 12 October 2026 lists Costa Rica, Guyana, Panama and Trinidad and Tobago as high income, against the high-income exclusion stated in regions.py, and a change to the LAC list waits on Jason and Adrian at the publication of the LAC region page (notes line 2264).
