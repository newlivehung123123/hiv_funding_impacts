Reconciliation

Where two or more sources publish a figure for the same indicator, country and year, the panel keeps the figure from every source and does not pick a single best figure. The reconciliation page compares the figures from different sources. A matched figure is a figure from a first source set beside a figure from a second source for the same indicator, country, year, population group, sex and age group. A comparison of matched figures is useful only when the reader knows whether the two sources measure separately, republish the same estimate, or differ in scope.

Derived

{{ recon.derived_fmt }} comparisons

The two sources publish the same underlying estimate. UNAIDS, the WHO Global Health Observatory and the World Bank largely republish the output of Spectrum, the modelling software countries use for the annual UNAIDS estimates, so agreement between UNAIDS, WHO and the World Bank is arithmetic. Counting the agreement as confirmation from separate sources would overstate the confidence in the estimate.

Subset

{{ recon.subset_fmt }} comparisons

The source with the smaller figure covers only part of the population or the programme that the source with the larger figure covers. PEPFAR reports the results of the PEPFAR programme, not the national total, so a gap between a PEPFAR figure and a national figure is expected and is not a contradiction.

Independent

{{ recon.independent_fmt }} comparisons

The two sources come from separate measurement systems, for example a national programme register and a modelled estimate, or the OECD record of United States disbursements and the record the United States government publishes. Only for independent sources does a difference between two matched figures carry information.

A further {{ recon.insufficient|count }} comparisons come from source pairs with fewer than {{ recon.thr.min_cells }} matched figures, and the panel does not classify a source pair with fewer than {{ recon.thr.min_cells }} matched figures.

How every source pair was classified

A fixed statistical rule, with {{ recon.n_declared|count }} pairs classified from documents

The panel classifies every source pair from the matched figures of the pair and prints the basis for the classification beside the classification, so a reader can check the result. The thresholds were fixed before any pair was classified. Two sources are treated as publishing the same estimate when the two sources agree within {{ recon.thr.derived_band }} on at least {{ recon.thr.derived_share }} of matched figures and the median difference is under {{ recon.thr.derived_med }}. A source is treated as covering part of what a second source covers when the first source gives the smaller figure on at least {{ recon.thr.subset_dir }} of matched figures and the median ratio of the smaller figure to the larger figure is {{ recon.thr.subset_ratio }} or below. A source pair with fewer than {{ recon.thr.min_cells }} matched figures is not classified. {{ recon.n_declared|count(cap=True) }} source pairs are classified from documents instead of by the rule, because the documents show that the two sources print the same national count, and the Basis column of a pair classified from documents names the documents.
