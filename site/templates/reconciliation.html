When there are multiple values for the same measurement unit, the panel keeps every source's own figure but not selectively displays a single best value. This page compares them. The comparison is only worth reading once you know what kind of comparison it represents.

Independent

{{ recon.independent_fmt }} comparisons

Separate measurement systems: Such as a national programme register against a modelled estimate, or OECD donor reporting against United States self-reporting. Only here that data differences in the same measurement unit carry information.

How each verdict was reached

Inferred from the data, not assigned by hand

Each source pair is classified from its own overlapping cells and the evidence is written out alongside the verdict, so it can be checked or overridden. The thresholds are fixed in advance. We treat two sources as sharing an estimate when they agree within {{ recon.thr.derived_band }} on at least {{ recon.thr.derived_share }} of cells and the typical difference is under {{ recon.thr.derived_med }}; we treat one as a subset of the other when it is the smaller figure in at least {{ recon.thr.subset_dir }} of cells and the median ratio passes {{ recon.thr.subset_ratio }}; we do not classify at all when there are fewer than {{ recon.thr.min_cells }} cells.

Cells below the materiality floor for their unit, or where one side is zero or negative, are set aside instead of being counted as disagreements.
