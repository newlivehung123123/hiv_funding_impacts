Phase 1.1  ·  Southeast Asia

What happens to HIV services when the funding is withdrawn

The United States has restructured its global health assistance under the America First Global Health Strategy. This project brings together the published record of what follows, including donor funding, epidemic estimates, treatment coverage, and what the services themselves report. Our project aim is to measure the effect on HIV programmes in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). Southeast Asia is covered first, and we will expand the data coverage to the rest of the developing world.

Reconciliation

Most agreement between sources is republication, not corroboration.

UNAIDS, the WHO Global Health Observatory and the World Bank largely publish the same modelled estimate. When they agree, that agreement is arithmetic. Treating it as independent confirmation would manufacture confidence the data does not support.

Of {{ recon.n_total_fmt }} pairwise comparisons across the panel, {{ recon.derived_pct }}% fall between sources that share an underlying estimate. Only {{ recon.independent_fmt }} comparisons, on {{ recon.n_independent_pairs|count }} source pairs, are between separate measurement systems, where the data differences carry meaningful information.

Every classification is inferred from the data and written out with the evidence behind it (e.g., the number of overlapping cells, the median relative difference, the agreement share and the direction consistency). Each one can be checked or overridden.

How the panel is built

Phase 2 feasibility

Structured sources only

Figures come from machine-readable feeds and from published tables. Prose is never parsed for numbers and no language model is used anywhere in the pipeline. Where a country does not publish in a usable form, the site says so instead of estimating. Such an approach is designed to maximise data accuracy and avoid data contamination.

Read at source where possible

National programme reporting is the primary record; the international aggregators are the cross-check. Four Southeast Asian programmes are now read directly. The rest of the region is covered by the aggregator layer alone, and each country page states which applies.

Uncertainty is kept

Modelled estimates carry confidence bounds and those bounds are stored and drawn. Reporting periods that are still open are recorded, because a partial year plotted as a complete one results in measurement inaccuracy, as only provisional but not full funding in a given year is measured.
